# Pulseframe Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 October 2026

Pulseframe turns your music into beat-reactive videos. It is built to work on your phone, and it does not collect personal data about you. This policy explains exactly what the app touches and why.

## What the app uses, and why

- **Songs you pick.** When you choose a song, Pulseframe reads it on your device to find the beats, kicks, snares and vocals that drive the visuals. The song stays on your phone and is never uploaded.
- **Photos and videos you pick.** If you choose a picture or video as a background, the app uses it only inside your design, on your device.
- **Mic mode (optional).** If you turn on Mic mode, Pulseframe listens to music playing around you so the visuals can react to it. It listens only while Mic mode is on and the app is open. The sound is analysed on the spot to detect beats and is never recorded, saved, or sent anywhere. You never need Mic mode to use your own songs.
- **Your designs and exports.** Projects, saved designs and settings are stored on your device. Videos you export are saved where you choose, such as your gallery.
- **Crash reports.** If the app crashes, a crash log is saved on your device. Nothing is sent automatically. On the next launch you can choose to copy or share it with us. Sharing is always your decision.

## What we don't collect

Pulseframe has no account sign-up, no advertising, and no analytics or tracking tools. We do not collect your name, contact details, location, contacts, device identifiers, or browsing activity, and we do not sell or share personal data.

## Purchases

Pulseframe Pro is bought through Google Play. Google handles the payment and your payment details. The app receives only a confirmation of what you've bought, so it can unlock Pro features. Google's handling of your data is covered by the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## Third parties

The only third party involved is Google Play, for purchases and for delivering the app. No other companies receive data from Pulseframe.

## Data retention and deletion

Everything Pulseframe stores is on your device. You can delete projects and designs in the app. Clearing the app's storage or uninstalling it removes all of its data. Videos you exported stay where you saved them until you delete them. You can manage or cancel subscriptions in Google Play.

## Children's privacy

Pulseframe is not directed at children under 13, and it does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

## Your rights

Because we don't collect or hold personal data about you, there is usually nothing for us to access, correct, or delete. If you've shared a crash report with us and want it deleted, contact us and we will delete it.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the date above and post the new version in the app and on this page.
